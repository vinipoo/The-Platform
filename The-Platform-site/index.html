the model

exchange

soft landing

A reciprocal model. Every founder who arrives at a partner hub gets a workstation, an intro meeting, a market overview and two or three relevant introductions. Theirs get the same here.

monthly

round tables

A monthly Zoom session with one international hub, for 3–5 Platform founders who want to go deep on a specific target market.

talent

inbound interns

We recruit international interns as skilled, free support for startups working out of The Platform.

cross-continent connections

Direct, fast introductions to VCs, investors, accelerators and corporates overseas.

knowledge exchange & delegations

We host and send international delegations, and swap programs and working methods with partner hubs.

who we are

welcome to the platform

The Platform is Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality's hub for entrepreneurship. We're based in Neve Shaanan, South Tel Aviv, and we back early-stage founders from day one.

We're operated by Tel Aviv Tech, the International Economic Department of Tel Aviv Global, a municipal corporation of the Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality. Tel Aviv Tech drives the city's whole tech ecosystem, from early-stage startups to the world's largest multinationals.

Founders get what they need to move fast: a professional environment and high-level resources, at no cost and no equity.
